  dalmoori-8.bdf

dalmoori-8.bdf is an 8px BDF conversion of the official Dalmoori glyph data.
Native 8-row glyphs are kept as exact pixel bitmaps instead of being
re-rasterized from the TTF, which preserves the original dot patterns.
The eight 16-row halfwidth glyphs are reduced by sampling their pixel
centers at the target size.
The source font is distributed by RanolP at:
  https://github.com/RanolP/dalmoori-font
